Earthquake Safety Guidelines

Before an Earthquake

Keep emergency telephone numbers for doctors, hospitals and police readily available.

Repair defective electrical wiring and leaking gas connections.

Keep heavy objects such as pictures and mirrors away from beds.

Follow the relevant BIS codes for earthquake-resistant building standards in your area.

Repair deep cracks in ceilings, walls and foundations.

During an Earthquake

When indoors, stay inside. Drop to the ground, take cover under a sturdy table or desk, and hold on until the shaking stops.

Stay away from windows, glass, exterior walls and objects that may fall.

When outdoors, move away from buildings, trees, streetlights and utility wires.

Drop to the ground, take cover and hold on until the shaking stops.

Do not use elevators during an earthquake.

After an Earthquake

Check yourself and others for injuries and provide first aid if necessary.

Be prepared for aftershocks and move to a safe open area if necessary.

Check for damaged electrical wiring and leaking gas connections. Do not use open flames if you suspect a gas leak.

If trapped under debris, cover your mouth with a handkerchief or clothing.

If trapped, tap on a pipe or wall to help rescuers locate you.
